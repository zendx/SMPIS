SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION (SRS) AND APPLICATION BLUEPRINT
School Management, Performance and Intelligence System (SMPIS)
Version: 1.0Date: August 30, 2026Prepared For: School ManagementDocument Type: Software Requirements Specification and Application Blueprint
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
1.1 Project Overview
The School Management, Performance and Intelligence System (SMPIS) is an integrated digital platform designed to manage, monitor, analyse, and improve all major areas of school operations.
The application will provide a single, centralized platform for managing:
Student Enrolment
Student Attendance
Staff Attendance
Fees Collection
Academic Performance
Curriculum Coverage
Student Discipline
Parent Satisfaction and Complaints
Staffing
Facilities and Assets
The system will serve as both an operational management platform and a management intelligence system.
Rather than maintaining separate spreadsheets, paper records, financial records, attendance registers, examination reports, and maintenance logs, the school will maintain a centralized database with role-based access.
The system will provide real-time dashboards and alerts to enable school management to make informed decisions.

30. STRATEGIC VISION
The long-term vision is to develop the application beyond a traditional School Management System.
The SMPIS should become a:
SCHOOL PERFORMANCE AND MANAGEMENT INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM
The system should answer important management questions such as:
Are student numbers growing?
Are parents satisfied?
Are students attending school?
Are fees being collected efficiently?
Which students require academic intervention?
Which subjects are underperforming?
Are teachers completing the curriculum?
Are staff meeting expectations?
Which facilities require investment?
Which operational issues require immediate management attention?
The ultimate objective is to give school leadership a real-time, data-driven view of the entire institution and enable proactive intervention before problems become major operational, financial, academic, or reputational risks.
